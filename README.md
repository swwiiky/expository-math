<h1>Expository</h1>

This repository is a personal collection of mathematical notes, part study journal, part expository writing.
My goal is to understand ideas deeply: not just how techniques work, but why they arise naturally and why they couldn’t really be otherwise.

<h3>Why This Exists</h3>

I understand mathematics best when I take the time to write things out carefully. Slowing down, asking simple questions, and following ideas back to their roots often reveals connections that aren’t obvious at first.

This repository is a place for that kind of thinking. The notes here reflect how I work through concepts for myself: trying to understand where definitions come from, what problems ideas are meant to address, and how different pieces fit together.

Sharing these notes is partly practical and partly generous. I find it useful to keep everything in one place, and if anyone else finds clarity or enjoyment in them, that’s a welcome bonus.

Writing is how I think, and this repository reflects that process.

<h3>Organization</h3>

The notes are grouped by topic, but they’re meant to be read independently. There’s no prescribed order; follow your curiosity. Each piece is written as a standalone essay, focusing on clarity and insight rather than completeness.

<h3>How to Read This</h3>

- Browse by topic
- Open any note that catches your interest
- No prior familiarity with the rest of the repository is expected
- The emphasis is on curiosity and understanding, not formal prerequisites.

<h3>Feedback & Discussion</h3>

If you spot an error, have a question, or want to push an idea further, feel free to open a GitHub issue.
Thoughtful discussion is always welcome.
